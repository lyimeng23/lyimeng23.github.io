Research Trajectory

From sensing the physical world to understanding and acting within it.

The agenda is a continuous progression in what an AI system can preserve, maintain, and do with physical evidence.

Published Foundations

Make physical evidence reliable.

  1. Multimodal sensing
  2. Cross-modal learning
  3. Physical representation

Hydra, Proteus, and Adonis establish complementary perception, knowledge transfer, and fine-grained physical-state recovery.

Current Research

Turn observations into a maintained spatial state.

  1. Persistent spatial state
  2. Dynamic world models
  3. Spatial reasoning

Current work extends sensing toward human-centered, dynamic spaces, where observations must remain useful across time and interaction.

Future Agenda

Predict change and support grounded action.

  1. Prediction
  2. Interaction
  3. Embodied decision & action

The long-term goal is an AI that can explain what is changing, anticipate what happens next, and choose a safe response.

The through-line: from sensing the physical world to understanding and acting within it.
