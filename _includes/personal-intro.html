A little context

A researcher working from sensing to spatial understanding.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science & Engineering at Michigan State University, advised by Dr. Zhichao Cao. My background in mathematics and mobile computing led me to a practical question: how can AI remain useful when the physical world is incomplete, noisy, and changing?

I build sensing and learning systems that connect multimodal observations to physical state. My long-term interests are in Spatial Intelligence, dynamic world understanding, and human-centered systems that can reason and act with physical context.
