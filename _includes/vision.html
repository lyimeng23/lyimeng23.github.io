Research Vision

One physical space. Five levels of understanding.

Today's foundation models are strong on static vision and language, but stable understanding of continuously changing, partially observable, multi-entity real environments — maintaining spatial state over time, respecting physical constraints, predicting, and acting grounded in that state — remains hard.

Spatial Intelligence, operationally

Start with incomplete multimodal observations. Build a persistent account of what is where and how it relates. Keep that account current as the world changes, then use it to reason, predict, and support safe action.

Future research agenda

Make the world model persistent, predictive, and actionable.

From isolated sensing → persistent spatial state → dynamic world models → spatial reasoning and prediction → embodied decision and action. Over the next five to ten years, this agenda asks how AI can know what is where, how things relate, what is changing, and what will happen next — then act on that understanding safely.
