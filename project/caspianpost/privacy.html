Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Welcome to CaspianPost. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our website or use our application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use CaspianPost, specifically when you connect your TikTok account, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: TikTok username and TikTok open_id.
  • Authentication: OAuth access tokens to securely interact with the TikTok API on your behalf.
  • Content Data: Uploaded video files and AI-generated captions created during your session.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect strictly for the following purposes:

  • To publish promotional videos to TikTok on your behalf, per your explicit request.
  • To generate AI-enhanced captions and optimize hashtags for your content.
  • To improve the overall user experience and performance of our service.

3. Data Storage and Security

We prioritize your data security. Your OAuth tokens are stored securely using industry-standard encryption. Video files uploaded to our platform are temporary and are deleted from our servers shortly after being successfully published to TikTok (or within a maximum of 24 hours). We do not permanently store your media files.

4. Third-Party Services

To provide our service, we integrate with the following third-party platform technologies:

  • TikTok API: Used for account connection and content publishing.
  • AI Model Providers: Used to generate intelligent captions and hashtag recommendations dynamically.

5. User Rights

You have full control over your data. At any time, you can:

  • Disconnect your TikTok account from CaspianPost via your TikTok settings or our platform.
  • Request full deletion of your account and associated data from our systems.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:

support@caspianpost.com
